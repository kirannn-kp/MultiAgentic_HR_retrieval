# ERD-002 Compliance Escalation Matrix

*Enterprise Rules & Directives (ERD) record | Owner: Group Compliance | (sample content, fictional)*

## Escalation areas
| Area | Trigger examples | Responsible team | SLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal | Termination, litigation, contract disputes | HR Legal | 2 working days |
| Compliance | Harassment, bribery, whistleblowing, data breach | Group Compliance / DPO | 1 working day |
| Tax | Cross-border work, taxable benefits, permanent establishment | Tax Department | 3 working days |

## Rules
1. Requests that touch an area above with a **HIGH** risk level are never answered automatically; a human expert must respond.
2. AI answers with a confidence score below 0.6 are routed to a human agent.
3. Every escalation creates a ServiceNow incident including the AI draft answer for human review.
