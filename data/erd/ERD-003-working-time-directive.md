# ERD-003 Working Time and Overtime Directive

*Enterprise Rules & Directives (ERD) record | Owner: HR Labour Relations | (sample content, fictional)*

## Working time
Regular weekly working time is **35 hours** for tariff employees and **40 hours** for non-tariff employees. Maximum daily working time is 10 hours; a rest period of 11 hours between working days is mandatory.

## Overtime
Overtime must be ordered in advance by the manager and is recorded on the working-time account. Time-off in lieu is preferred to payment. Collective overtime requires works council consent (see ERD-001).

## Time account limits
The flexible time account may hold between -50 and +150 hours. Above +100 hours the manager must agree a reduction plan with the employee.
