# Annual Leave and Special Leave Policy

*Owner: HR Policies | Version 2.8 | (sample content, fictional)*

## Annual leave entitlement
Full-time employees are entitled to **30 vacation days per calendar year** (5-day week). Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement. New joiners receive 1/12 of the annual entitlement for each full month of employment.

## Carry-over
Unused vacation can be carried over to the next year and must be taken **by 31 March**. After this date the carried-over days expire unless the employee was prevented from taking leave due to illness or operational reasons.

## Requesting leave
Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 5 days. The manager approves or rejects within 5 working days.

## Cash payout
Vacation cannot be paid out in cash while the employment contract is active. Payout is only possible at the end of employment for statutory leave days that could not be taken.

## Special leave
Paid special leave: own wedding 1 day, birth of a child 1 day, death of a close relative up to 2 days, relocation 1 day.

## Parental leave
Parental leave follows statutory rules (up to 36 months per child). Employees notify HR at least 7 weeks before the planned start. Part-time work during parental leave of up to 32 hours per week is possible by agreement.
