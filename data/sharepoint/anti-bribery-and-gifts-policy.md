# Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy

*Owner: Group Compliance | Version 3.1 | (sample content, fictional)*

## Principle
The company does not tolerate bribery or corruption in any form, whether involving public officials or private business partners.

## Gifts and hospitality limits
Employees may accept or offer gifts and hospitality only if they are reasonable, transparent and customary. The value limit is **EUR 50 per gift** and **EUR 150 per hospitality event** per person and year per business partner. Cash or cash equivalents (such as vouchers) are never allowed.

## Approval and register
Anything above the limits requires written pre-approval from Group Compliance. All gifts and hospitality above EUR 25 must be recorded in the compliance register within 5 working days.

## Public officials
Gifts, hospitality or other benefits to public officials are prohibited without prior written approval of Group Compliance.

## Red flags
Requests for unusual payments, payments to third countries or accounts not matching the contract party, and excessive commissions must be escalated to Group Compliance **immediately**.

## Consequences
Violations may lead to disciplinary action up to termination and to criminal liability for the individual and the company.
