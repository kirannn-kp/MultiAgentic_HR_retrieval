# Code of Conduct

*Owner: Group Compliance | Version 5.0 | Applies to all employees worldwide (sample content, fictional)*

## Our principles
We act lawfully, honestly and respectfully. Every employee is responsible for knowing and following this Code, the policies that apply to their role and applicable laws.

## Speaking up
Employees who observe or suspect a violation must report it through their manager, Group Compliance or the whistleblowing channel (see Whistleblowing Policy). Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself a violation.

## Gifts and hospitality
Gifts and hospitality must be modest, infrequent and never intended to influence a business decision. See the Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy for limits and approval rules.

## Conflicts of interest
Employees must avoid situations in which personal interests conflict with those of the company and must disclose potential conflicts to their manager and Group Compliance. See the Conflict of Interest Policy.

## Confidential information and data protection
Confidential company information and personal data must only be used for legitimate business purposes and protected against unauthorised access. See the Employee Data Privacy Guideline.

## Consequences
Violations may lead to disciplinary measures up to and including termination of employment, and to civil or criminal liability. Disciplinary measures are decided together with HR Legal and, where applicable, the works council.

## Questions
Employees with questions about the Code can contact Group Compliance (compliance@mb-enterprise.example). Questions are answered within 2 working days.
