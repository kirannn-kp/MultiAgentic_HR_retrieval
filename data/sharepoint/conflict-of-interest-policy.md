# Conflict of Interest and Secondary Employment Policy

*Owner: Group Compliance / HR Policies | Version 1.7 | (sample content, fictional)*

## Disclosure duty
Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest in writing to their manager and Group Compliance **before** the situation arises, for example when a close relative works for a supplier or when the employee holds a financial interest in a competitor or business partner.

## Secondary employment
Secondary employment requires prior written approval from HR if it could affect working time, performance or the company's interests. Work for competitors, suppliers or customers is generally not approved. Approval is decided within 10 working days; unpaid volunteer work and minor private activities do not require approval.

## Board and advisory roles
Roles in external boards, advisory councils or political offices must be reported to Group Compliance. Roles in listed companies or competitors require approval of the executive board.

## Decisions involving relatives
Employees must not take part in hiring, supervision, evaluation or contracting decisions involving relatives or close personal relationships. A relationship between a manager and a direct report must be disclosed to HR.

## Consequences
Undisclosed conflicts can lead to disciplinary measures including termination.
