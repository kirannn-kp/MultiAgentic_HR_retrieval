# Employee Data Privacy Guideline

*Owner: Data Protection Office (DPO) | Version 1.9 | (sample content, fictional)*

## Principles
Personal employee data may only be processed for defined HR purposes, must be limited to what is necessary and must be protected against unauthorised access.

## Access
HR staff access employee master data on a need-to-know basis. Managers see only data of their direct reports. Sharing employee data with third parties requires a data processing agreement.

## Data subject requests
Requests for access, correction or deletion of personal data (GDPR articles 15-17) must be forwarded to the **Data Protection Office within 2 working days**. Employees must not answer such requests themselves. The legal response deadline is one month.

## Data breaches
A suspected personal-data breach must be reported to the DPO **immediately, at the latest within 24 hours**. The DPO decides on notification of the supervisory authority within 72 hours.

## Special categories
Health data, religion, union membership and biometric data are special-category data. Processing requires explicit legal basis and prior approval by the DPO.

## Monitoring
Monitoring of employee activity (e-mail, chat, location) is prohibited unless agreed with the works council and approved by the DPO.
