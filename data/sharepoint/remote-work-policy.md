# Remote Work Policy

*Owner: HR Policies | Version 3.2 | Applies to all employees in Germany (sample content, fictional)*

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** if their role allows it and their manager approves the arrangement in the HR portal. Trainees and employees in their probation period may work remotely up to 1 day per week.

## Equipment and costs
The company provides a laptop, a headset and a monitor. A monthly home-office allowance of **EUR 25** is paid with the salary. Employees are responsible for a suitable workplace and a stable internet connection.

## Working time and availability
Remote work does not change contractual working time. Employees must be reachable during core hours (09:30-15:30) and record working time in the time-tracking system.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is only permitted for a maximum of **20 working days per calendar year**, in EU/EEA countries only, and requires prior written approval from HR. Longer stays or stays outside the EU/EEA can create tax and social-security obligations and permanent-establishment risk for the company and must be assessed by HR Legal and Tax before approval.

## Security
Company data may only be processed on company-managed devices. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN enabled.
