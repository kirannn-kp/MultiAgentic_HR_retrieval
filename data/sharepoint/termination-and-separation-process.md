# Termination and Separation Process

*Owner: HR Legal | Version 2.1 | Restricted - HR Business Partners only (sample content, fictional)*

## Scope
This process covers dismissals, mutual termination agreements and severance. **Every case must be handled by HR Legal** - managers and HR Business Partners must not communicate termination intentions before HR Legal approval.

## Notice periods
Statutory and contractual notice periods apply: 4 weeks to the 15th or end of a month as a minimum; extended notice periods depend on length of service.

## Severance
There is no automatic entitlement to severance. Typical severance guidance in negotiated agreements is 0.5 months' gross salary per year of service, subject to approval by HR Legal and the works council hearing process.

## Works council
The works council must be heard before every termination. A termination without hearing is invalid.

## Disciplinary measures
Warnings and disciplinary measures require documentation, prior consultation with HR Legal and, where applicable, the works council.
