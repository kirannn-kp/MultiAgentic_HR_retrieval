# Whistleblowing Policy

*Owner: Group Compliance | Version 2.3 | (sample content, fictional)*

## Scope
This policy covers reports of suspected violations of law, the Code of Conduct or internal policies, including fraud, bribery and corruption, harassment and discrimination, health and safety violations and data protection breaches.

## Reporting channels
Reports can be made (1) to the employee's manager, (2) to Group Compliance, or (3) through the independent whistleblowing hotline, which is available 24/7 in all major languages and allows **anonymous** reporting.

## Handling of reports
Group Compliance confirms receipt of a report **within 7 days** and gives feedback on the follow-up measures **within 3 months**. Reports are investigated impartially, confidentially and only by authorised persons.

## Protection from retaliation
Whistleblowers acting in good faith are protected. Dismissal, demotion, threats, bullying or any other detriment because of a report is prohibited. Retaliation allegations are handled by Group Compliance together with HR Legal.

## External reporting
Employees may also report to the competent external authority under the applicable whistleblower protection law. They are not required to report internally first.

## Confidentiality
The identity of the whistleblower and of persons concerned may only be disclosed to those who need to know for the investigation, or where the law requires disclosure.
